### 👋 About Me

I'm an **AI/ML engineer and founder** specializing in **computer vision**, **anomaly detection**, and **diffusion models** — bridging cutting-edge research with production systems.

My M.Sc. thesis at [JKU Linz](https://www.jku.at) explored **conditional diffusion models for out-of-distribution detection**, and my industrial work at [PROFACTOR GmbH](https://www.profactor.at) reached **98.4% accuracy** on real-world defect detection. That same research-to-product reflex led me to found **[Faultrix](https://faultrix.com)** — an AI SaaS that turns building photos into ÖNORM-compliant quality-control reports in under a minute.
